# Fresh research protocol

Research after core intake and before stack selection and planning on every
generation. First read the current series documents. Then make generic external
queries scoped to the selected surface and task shape only: never send
repository paths, source, private/company/user content, exercise defects, or
verbatim user answers outside the repository.

Prefer current primary sources and verify the real publication date. The
generated `for-interviewer/research-notes.md` records each source URL, access
date, source tier, and one supported claim. Examples can guide a search but are
not evergreen truth: CoderPad's 2026 technology hiring survey is directional
Tier C; Stack Overflow's 2025 survey and State of JS 2025 are Tier B; a SIOP
summary of Sackett et al. (2022) structured-interview/work-sample evidence is
Tier-A-quality secondary evidence; and Jacob Kaplan-Moss's pair-programming
caution is Tier C.

| Tier | Source                                                                            | Permitted use                               |
| ---- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| A    | Official framework/runtime documentation; peer-reviewed or reputable I/O research | Supports a design rule                      |
| B    | Large primary surveys with methodology                                            | Direction and adoption only                 |
| C    | Vendor surveys and practitioner posts                                             | Direction only; never the sole design basis |

At most five cited formula deltas may result. Research may tune emphasis, a
defect, or probe wording; it never changes selected seniority, type, length, or
domain. Stop if a proposed design-rule delta lacks Tier A or B support.

Research stack adoption separately from interview design. Use current primary
usage surveys for popularity and official framework/runtime documentation for
support status, compatibility, and recommended setup. Present three to five
surface-compatible stack options, always including the nearest current-series
stack. Verify each option's current local testing setup from official
documentation and include its test runner and exact command in the shortlist.
Reject options that depend on unavailable hosted infrastructure for basic
tests. Record all options and their sources; do not describe a stack as popular
from a single vendor post, search-result ranking, or headline. The user selects
the stack after seeing the shortlist.
